About JIC

Faith. Community. Service.

Serving Birmingham from Woodlands Road with prayer, learning and community support.

About Us

Jamatia Islamic Centre is a welcoming masjid and community centre dedicated to worship, Islamic learning and practical support for local families. Our aim is to provide a trusted space where people of all ages can strengthen their faith and contribute positively to the wider community.

Our Mission

To provide excellent religious, educational and community services rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah, delivered with compassion, integrity and openness.
